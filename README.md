# Sdjtravels

Build a mobile-first landing page for a travel platform for young Indian travellers (22-35), including solo women. Headline: 'Tell us what you want from a trip. We find where to go, show you the real price, and make sure you can actually get there.' Hero section with a large text box where users describe a trip in their own words, with the placeholder 'Five days in November, under ₹80,000, warm weather, good food, safe for a solo woman, no visa hassle'. Below it, three feature cards: Trip Score (rates destinations on social, safety, fitness and cultural factors), All-in Price (flight, baggage and visa fees in one number), and Booking Checks (visa, passport validity and layover checked before you pay). Add a three-step how-it-works section, a sample Trip Score card, and a waiting-list email sign-up. Style: warm, trustworthy, clean and modern, with a sunset-orange and deep-teal palette and rounded cards. Use 'Wayfind' as a placeholder name. Don't mention any competitors.
